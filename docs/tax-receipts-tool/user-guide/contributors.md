# Contributors

[Back to the guide](README.md)

Contributors are the people who give. Their name and address print on
receipts and go to EO, so they must be right.

## Finding a contributor

**Contributors** has a search box: type at least two letters of a name or
email. Open a contributor to see:

- **Details**: name, email, address, and contributor type (always
  Individual: Ontario accepts contributions from individuals only).
- **Contributions**: everything they have given, with period, entity,
  amount, status, and what is left to receipt. With *Issue receipts*, tick
  two or more to issue one receipt for all of them (see
  [Combined receipts](issuing-receipts.md#combined-receipts)).
- **Change history**: every change to their record, with before and after.

## Adding a contributor

Needs *Add and edit contributors*. Add a contributor from:

- **Contributors > Add a contributor**;
- the donor picker when [recording a payment](contributions-and-payments.md#recording-a-payment-by-hand);
- a contribution's detail page.

Enter the name, email, and address. If the tool finds a likely duplicate
(the same email, or the same name and postal code), it shows the match and
asks you to confirm before creating a second record. Use the existing one
if it is the same person.

## Editing a contributor

Open the contributor (or use **edit donor** on a contribution) and choose
**Edit contributor**. Give a reason. Editing re-checks that contributor's
active contributions, so fixing an address can close address problems in
the [work queue](work-queue.md) straight away.

A correction to a name or address does not change a receipt already issued:
the receipt keeps the address it was printed with. To put the corrected
details on a receipt, see [Corrections](corrections.md#receipt-actions).

## Qomon and contributor records

Who owns a contact record depends on whether the tool is connected to
Qomon:

- **Connected to Qomon.** Qomon is the home of contact records. A new
  contributor is created in Qomon first and then copied into the tool. An
  edit changes only the fields you changed in Qomon, then copies back what
  Qomon holds. If Qomon refuses the change, nothing changes in the tool
  either.
- **Not connected.** The tool owns its contacts and edits them directly.

A contact that came from Qomon cannot be edited while the connection is
off. A contact created in the tool with no Qomon link can always be
edited.

**Refresh donor from Qomon** (on a contribution's detail page) pulls the
latest copy of a Qomon contact into the tool, for example after someone
fixed an address in Qomon directly.

## Duplicate records

If two records turn out to be the same person, merge them with the
**merge** [correction](corrections.md#contribution-corrections). The merged
record stops being offered as a donor, and its contributions move to the
surviving record. When Qomon is connected, merge the contacts in Qomon too.
